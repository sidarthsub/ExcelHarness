# User context

You're a VC associate allocating a follow-on pool to prior-round
investors. You want the allocation math to follow the standard
pro-rata convention.

## Defaults you would pick

- Pro-rata by prior dollar investment (not ownership %).
- Don't round — fractional dollars are fine.
- Formula references only — no literal numeric answers.

## Things you do NOT know or care about

- Cell formatting or number precision beyond a handful of decimals.
- Whether the formulas use direct cell refs or named ranges.
- Whether intermediate totals are visible — the inputs are already
  laid out on the `Calc` sheet and you just want the three answers
  in `B10:B12`.
