# LBO — Reed Industries

## Transaction
- Entry date: start of Year 1
- Purchase price: $500,000,000 enterprise value (EV)
- Entry EBITDA: $50,000,000 (10.0x EV/EBITDA)
- Transaction fees: $10,000,000 (all paid at close)

## Financing (Sources)
- Term Loan B (TLB): $250,000,000 at 7.0% interest
- Mezzanine: $50,000,000 at 10.0% interest
- Sponsor equity: plug (= remainder of uses)

## Operating assumptions
- EBITDA growth: 8% per year
- Capex = 4% of revenue; revenue at entry = $250M, growing 6% per year
- Tax rate: 25%
- D&A = $12M flat per year
- Working capital changes: zero (simplified)

## Debt behavior
- TLB: 100% cash sweep (all excess FCF pays down TLB after interest and mandatory amort). Mandatory amort = 1% of original balance per year.
- Mezzanine: no amortization; bullet maturity at exit.

## Hold period
- 5 years. Exit at end of Year 5.
- Exit multiple: 11.0x trailing EBITDA (Year 5 EBITDA).
- Net debt at exit: TLB remaining + Mezzanine balance, minus excess cash (assume zero, so just sum debt balances).

## Returns
- Sponsor exit proceeds = exit_equity_value = enterprise value at exit - net debt at exit.
- Sponsor MOIC = exit_proceeds / initial_sponsor_equity.
- Sponsor IRR = (exit_proceeds / initial_sponsor_equity)^(1/5) - 1.
