# Rivet Labs — Series A round terms

- **Pre-money valuation:** $40,000,000
- **Round size:** $15,000,000
- **Lead investor:** Meridian Capital — takes the full $15M as lead.
- **Option pool target:** 10% of post-money fully diluted shares, pre-money
  inclusion (existing holders absorb; target is net of existing unallocated).
- **Pre-round unallocated pool:** 500,000 shares (from the input cap table;
  counts toward the 10% target).

Post-money fully diluted shares = pre-round FD + SAFE shares + new investor
shares + new option pool top-up.
