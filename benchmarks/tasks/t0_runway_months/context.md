# User context

You are the finance lead forecasting runway for a board deck. The company
has a known step-up in monthly burn after month 6 (from a planned hiring
wave) and you want a clean, formula-driven view of how long cash lasts.

## Defaults you would pick

- Fractional months are fine — don't round or floor.
- Pure burn analysis — don't include revenue.
- Formulas only — every output cell should reference the inputs, not a
  hardcoded literal.

## Things you do NOT know or care about

- Number precision beyond a handful of decimals.
- Cell formatting.
- Whether intermediate cells show up alongside the answer — just want the
  right numbers in B9, B10, B11.
