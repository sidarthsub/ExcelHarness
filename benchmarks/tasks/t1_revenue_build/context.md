# User context

You are an FP&A analyst building a monthly revenue build.

## Defaults you'd pick

- Flat price (no escalation) — $50 across all 12 months.
- Seasonality fractions sum to 1.0 — verify with a SUM formula in the totals row.
- Formulas reference the `annual_units` and `price` cells — don't hardcode
  monthly units.

## Things you do NOT care about

- Exact column widths or colors beyond the title row.
- Whether the parameters area is labeled in a specific way, as long as the
  monthly units/price formulas reference it.
